Dropper Dash

How to play
  • Steer
    Drag anywhere on the screen. On a keyboard, use WASD or the arrow keys.
  • Dodge
    Touching a block sends you back to the top of that map. The clock keeps running.
  • Splash
    Land in the water at the bottom to move on to the next map.
  • Race the top 10
    The numbered markers on the right are this world's top 10 runs. Beat a time to take its spot.
  • Share worlds
    Send a seed or link and a friend races the exact same five maps.
